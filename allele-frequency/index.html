Worksheet calculation reference
  • Genotype frequency = genotype count ÷ total individuals (N).
  • A copies = 2 × AA count + Aa count. The frequency of A is p = A copies ÷ (2 × N).
  • a copies = 2 × aa count + Aa count. The frequency of a is q = a copies ÷ (2 × N).
  • Under Hardy–Weinberg expectations, the genotype frequencies for AA, Aa, and aa are p², 2pq, and q². Also, p + q = 1.
  • With complete dominance, AA and Aa show the dominant trait. Only aa shows the recessive trait.
